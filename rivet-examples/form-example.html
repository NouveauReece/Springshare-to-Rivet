Please RSVP for the 2026 INCEP Annual Convening

Event Information

When: Wednesday, June 24, 2026

Time: 8:30am - 3:00pm ET

Where: Indiana University Bloomington - Cook Center for Public Arts and Humanities (formerly known as Maxwell Hall) 750 E. Kirkwood Ave. Bloomington, IN 47405

Focus: Shaping a sustainable structure for participation, shared leadership, and practice across Indiana.

The 2026 INCEP Convening will feature a full-day practice-centered experience that combines relevant updates (state and national) with meaningful opportunities for connection and exchange.

The Convening will also include a vote on the revised INCEP bylaws and launch of the student competition for designing the new INCEP logo!

What to Expect: The 2026 INCEP Convening will include:

  • Bylaws Vote (in person): Vote on updated INCEP bylaws to support our next phase
  • New opportunities to engage: Learn about practical, manageable, and impactful ways to participate in INCEP
  • Sharing Practic Roundtables Session: Exchange innovative practices and strategies with other experts and practitioners
  • Networking Breaks: Chat with colleagues over delicious snacks
  • Program Info and Swag Exchange: Share program literature and swag with colleagues across the state
  • Student Engagement Initiative: Be there as we launch the INCEP student logo design competition

More Info:

  • Registration cost: Free!
  • Parking: Free!
  • Breakfast and lunch provided.
  • Coffee, tea, and water available throughout the day.

We are excited to welcome you to IU Bloomington on June 24 for the 2026 INCEP Convening!

Sharing Practice Roundtables

During the 2026 INCEP Convening, we will highlight innovative dual credit practices from across Indiana.

Sharing Practice Roundtables are informal, discussion-based sessions where participants share innovative approaches, models, or lessons learned in dual credit. These sessions are designed to support practical exchange and peer learning across institutions.

Expectations for Roundtable Leaders Leaders of Sharing Practice Roundtables will be expected to:

  • Lead a small-group discussion (15 minutes)
  • Share an innovative dual credit or early college practice, model, or approach
  • Bring a one-page handout or brief resource for participants
    • Please bring ~20 printed copies of your handout or brief. If accepted, we will also request a digital version for broader sharing with INCEP members and convening attendees.
  • Submit a short proposal (see below)

To submit a Sharing Practice Roundtable proposal, please provide a 250–500 word description of the session you would like to lead. (Please include the context, the practice you will share, and what participants will gain.)

Program Info & Swag Exchange

This year, we will host a Program Info & Swag Exchange designed to help participants learn about each other’s dual credit and early college programs and explore opportunities for collaboration across Indiana.

Through this exchange, we hope participants leave with new ideas, connections, and opportunities to collaborate across institutions.

We encourage participants to bring both:

  • Program literature (e.g., a flyer, one-pager, or resource), and
  • A small item or giveaway that represents your institution
  • Please plan to bring approximately 60 copies/items.
